# Master Roadmap

Baseline: ADR 0001 scope. Estimates are calendar weeks for solo founder plus Claude; confidence in `PHASE_0_FINDINGS.md`.

## Pilot MVP (target: within 6 months)
| Phase | Weeks | Exit criteria (gate) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Product and architecture | 2 to 3 | Docs approved |
| 2 Foundation | 4 to 6 | Sign in, tenant switch, permission-driven nav on staging; isolation tests green; CI green; restore drill done |
| 3 Communities and residents | 3 to 4 | Ops can create the pilot community; a resident can join, add family, children |
| 4 Vendors (ops-assisted) | 3 to 4 | 15 vendors entered with offerings; vendor can log in and see inquiries |
| 5 Requests and operations | 5 to 6 | Resident submits a request; ops works it to Approved with notes and SLA timers |
| 6 Matching and booking | 4 to 5 | Full loop: request, match, inquiry, quote, compare, shortlist, confirm, complete, review |
| Pilot go-live checklist | 1 to 2 | Security review, accessibility pass, legal docs, backups, support process |

## After pilot data (re-prioritize from evidence)
7 Discovery catalog (self-serve browse and recommendations), 9 Rentals, 8 Events, 10 Financial engine and payments, 11 AI, 12 Analytics, 13 Commerce, 14 Production hardening at scale.
Default order is by expected business value; pilot data may change it.

## Change control
Anything added to the MVP boundary requires: a written reason, the item it displaces, and an entry in `DEVELOPMENT_PROGRESS.md`.
